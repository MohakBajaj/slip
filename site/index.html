__FAQ_PRICE__

is jot only for macos?

yes. jot is a macos app.

which permissions does jot ask for?
  • microphone. jot hears you dictate.
  • accessibility. jot pastes the text into the app you're in.
  • input monitoring. the fn hold works while another app is in front.
  • screen recording. jot captures the screen when you annotate.
where does my data live?

your notes are files in a local folder you own, on your mac. transcription with parakeet, and cleanup with apple's on-device model, stay on the machine. pick a cloud model for either one, and that part is sent to the model you chose.
